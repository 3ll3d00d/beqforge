Every title's featured candidate, redrawn from its .run.json.gz record via tools/render_ledger.py — no rerun. Recovered fraction and the uncalibrated evidence score are explained below the fold before the numbers, not after.

Recovered fraction

How much of the mix's own measured shortfall the evidence licensed the fitter to attempt — not how well the cascade hit its target (it hits it within ~1 dB regardless). A ratio of the priced target to the raw deficit, summed over the band where there's a real deficit to speak of.

Σ priced target ÷ Σ max(−before_db, 0)

Evidence score (uncalibrated)

Conditional precision of temporal peak–quiet contrast, weighted by requested boost. Quiet frames must represent additive noise; tracking must not be leakage. Neither assumption is proved by this score. Mastering causality remains unavailable. Legacy records retain their original score and are labelled in their notes.

weighted max(1 − z SE / contrast, 0)

Shaping fraction

Share of the cascade's peak gain sitting below filter_floor_hz — a descriptive level-invariance boundary. This legacy measure does not separate mastering from source coloration; plateau-relative correction remains a shaping preference throughout.
